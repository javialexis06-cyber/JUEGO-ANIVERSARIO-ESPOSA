# Guide:Defeating the Sketamari
< Guides
The Sketamari can be one of the most difficult parts of progression, as defeating it is required to obtain the  Forbidden Scrolls of Morbane. However, there are plenty of strategies the player can use to make approaching this key boss easier. 
Since the Sketamari grows by absorbing the monsters it touches, but cannot grow when the player is further away from it, making it ideal to start the fight only after the player has an effective build. The player must balance their build with the number of enemies spawned by each wave, as later waves have more enemies, and thus, scale the Sketamari quicker.
- If the player chooses to, enabling  Limit Break then waiting until the end of the run, when their build has plenty of levels, can help.
-  Wicked Season (XIII) can help with getting more levels of Limit Break.
The player can prevent the Sketamari from getting too powerful by running away. The exact distance is 60,000 Units, but 1 Tileset on the pause menu map works as well.
If the player has the  Curse PowerUp enabled, it should be disabled and/or refunded temporarily. Other sources of Curse, such as  Skull O'Maniac and the final level of  Torrona's Box should also be avoided.
- Every facet of Curse contributes to making this fight more difficult. While the Health increase is self-explanatory, more and faster enemies leads to the Sketamari scaling even quicker.
 Hurry Mode is not recommended due to increasing the Timer Speed, thus increasing how fast the Bone Zone's Health Bonus ramps up, while also generally being a loss in Experience gain. On the other hand, Hyper Mode is recommended, since they player must travel to the Sketamari, on top of this option generally increasing Experience gains.
Generally, the player should focus on builds and weapons with large amount of  Area to prevent the Sketamari from absorbing enemies. However, builds with immense raw damage output work as well.
Some general options include:

# Guide:Gold farming
< Guides
## General Notes
- Gold Farming prior to defeating  The Directer in Eudaimonia M. is generally unrecommended. This is especially because the player will not have access to inverse mode before this point, which not only triples Gold gain, but also allows for an extra  Arcana to be purchased. However, if the player owns certain DLCs, then obtaining Gold prior to this point can be much easier.
- Gold gain in the endgame will always depend on framerate. Plenty of mechanics, especially levelling up, are faster with higher FPS.
- Generally, it is not recommended to enable  Hurry Mode, due to relative scaling and less XP.
- However, the speed up from  Roast Chicken with a Clock in the Middle is okay to use since it is a global speed up, instead of just the timer.
- After obtaining 5,000  Golden Eggs across any number of characters, the  Eggman will appear in Moongolow, allowing the player to spend all of their Gold on Eggs at once.
- After obtaining 5,000  Golden Eggs on a single character,  The Healer will also appear in Moongolow, allowing the player to remove 100  Golden Eggs at a time.
- At 25,000 Eggs on a single character, The Healer will allow the player to specifically remove all  Golden Eggs that provide  Move Speed.
- The player does not need to have Eggs enabled for The Healer to appear, which is relevant to the above option.
- Excessive Level Ups make it difficult, if not impossible, to pick up  Treasure Chests and Stage Items. However, the "Popups Follow Priority" Option makes the aforementioned Popups register regardless of queued Level Ups.
- This enables the use of Evolutions and more  Arcana in "high Egg" builds, as well as builds utilizing  Sammy.
- Sealing is heavily utilized in Gold farming. This is done mostly to eliminate  Orologion, which can drastically slow down Gold income by lowering the number of enemies killed each second.
- Shorter runs tend to be better than longer ones as the player gets more Eggs. Not only will the player's Greed not change much over long runs with high eggs, but lag also hurts gold gain. Shorter runs also allow the player to spend more time farming with higher Greed, as they can purchase Golden Eggs more often.
## Why not Bone Zone?
While the Bone Zone has the highest stage gold bonus, there are many other factors that make other stages better. These include:
- Light sources not being able to spawn Rich Coin Bags.
- Worse enemy spawns along with enemies scaling over time, which matters more with lower Eggs.
- Moongolow having Moon Anforas, which drop extra gold.
- Most DLC stages, and Capella Magna, having a higher maximum for light sources.
## Survarots vs. Eggs
Since these Power Creep options are, generally, mutually exclusive, it can be tough to choose between the two. When starting out, Survarots outperform eggs by a large margin, allowing the player to get obscene amounts of stats on nearly any character. Once the player is nearing the Might/Area caps via eggs, then they should switch to farming with eggs. 
- However,  Secretino Dagsson, characters from Ante Chamber, the Ante Chamber stage, and  Bat Dragons allow the player to make use of both options, though, at high eggs, stat-based Survarot skills fall off.
Some of the most important (FOIL) Skills these cards can provide are the ability to generate light sources from collecting Gold, and the ability to start a  Gold Fever from killing enemies, providing a consistent source of Gold for any build. This will also produce less lag than  Greatest Jubilee, allowing the player to get more kills and level up more often thanks to a higher FPS. This skill is by far the most beneficial for gold farming and can be used in any run to make extra gold without going overkill.
The main downside of Survarots is that it can be relatively expensive to get the card you want early on, since getting a specific card is never guaranteed and it can take potentially millions of gold before it is seen. However, they can more than make up for it in terms of power and gold. 
This makes the Ante Chamber stage, and the characters from the DLC, some of, if not the best, options outside of  Sammy [DLC] and paid DLC. Currently, Mad Forest can also be a fair option at high egg counts thanks to the  Bat Dragon that can spawn if the player has the  Green Eyes Vizard, providing a single Survarot regardless of Power Creep settings.
## Gold Farming in the Early Game: 0 Eggs
While farming solely with the intent on accruing gold is not advised, there are multiple things the player can do to get more gold:
- Various  Arcana can vastly increase the player's gold gain, while not being too difficult to unlock. Ideally, the player should unlock as many as possible, but the follow two are the most important:
-  Disco of Gold (XV) gives the player easy access to  Gold Fevers.
-  Wicked Season (XIII) grants the player various stats that are useful for farming gold, namely  Greed.
- Moongolow and The Bone Zone are stages that exist for the purpose of Gold farming, with the latter being much more difficult with a higher stage gold bonus. The Bone Zone also has high numbers of Light sources.
- Easier stages that the player can repeat, like Il Molise and Inlaid Library, can also work.
- While it provides no offensive or defensive utility in a run, the  Greed Power Up can make getting all other Power UPs much much quicker, while being relatively cheap itself.
- Repetition, opening Treasure Chests, and obtaining new evolutions are where a large portion of early gold comes from, especially the former two.
-  Golden Eggs are not worth investing into at this stage. It can take hundreds to see a significant difference, costing the player hours of playtime or millions of gold that could be better spent.
Again, gold farming at this stage should be low priority. The quantities of gold required prior to fighting the Directer is relatively low to the point where the player can get all of the gold they need simply by playing and progressing through the game. 
## Gold Farming Right After the Directer: 0/Very Low Eggs
This section is for when the player unlocks and completes the final stage of progression, Eudaimonia Machine. This is when the player has the most important tools for Gold Farming at their disposal, and can relatively easily unlock any specifics. This is also the point at which gold becomes more relevant for purchasing more expensive powerups, items from the Merchant, and spending it on Survarots. 
The previous tips for farming in the early game still apply, especially in terms of Arcana. Once the player unlocks Darkana, both  Stake to Your Heart (0) and  Hail from the Future (X) can be a great pick for any run where the players wants extra gold, though they are essentially mutually exclusive as Stake to Your Heart (0) will prevent levelups on most characters.
## Eudaimonia M.: Quick and Easy - 0 Eggs
While far from the best, repeatedly clearing Eudaimonia Machine can be a passable gold farm once the player gets to this point, but is not recommended for more costly endeavors such as the later ranks of Seal.
Clearing the stage is relatively simple. All the player must do is survive, kill enemies, and collect  Experience. Strong characters such as  Mask of the Red Death can make clearing this stage easier. 
This fight can be clear in as little as 5 minutes, and rewards the player with at least 150,000 Gold with each successful attempt. 
This method can be used to get the player started with a few hundred  Golden Eggs for other methods, purchasing  Arcana in  Inverse Mode, or for the lower end of expensive PowerUps and Characters.
However,  Golden Eggs can not be used to improve Gold income from this method, as all  Golden Eggs are taken away at the beginning of the stage.

## Strategy, Other Notes, and Tips
- Much like Queen Sigma's build, all the player has to do is get the build up, kill enemies, and collect XP. Otherwise, they can remain AFK.
- Straying from a given build and taking more weapons than are recommended, being around 1-2 Max Weapons, is not recommended as it can lead to a decrease in Gold.
- These methods are never the most efficient, but they are the least active and almost always work, even at 0  Golden Eggs.
- At high  Golden Eggs,  Wicked Season (XIII) and  Hail from the Future (X) are the most recommended Arcana.
- As the player unlocks and purchases more ranks of Seal, sealing the rest of the pickups, and eventually every passive, can slightly increase Gold gain.
- At any amount of  Golden Eggs that are higher than 1,000,000, another option is to seal everything in the collection, take nothing from the Candybox, and instead rely on Victorian Horror (XVIII) for its greed-scaling damage. Make sure to turn Limit Break off, and pick "always coin bag".

## Weapons and Passives
It is not recommended for Sammy to use any weapon other than its starting weapon,  Vicious Hunger. Taking other weapons generally increases lag while decreasing Gold Income. 
- The only exception to this is  Paranormal Scan [DLC], which simply provides more stats while barely influencing  Limit Break.
Also, because of Sammy's ability to accrue tens of thousands of levels, most Passives have relatively little effect.
| Passive | Note
| Karoma's Mana | Provides relevant stats to Gold Farming, increasing enemy spawns and Gold Fever duration.
| Spinach | Minor damage boost.
| Bracer | Helps Vicious Hunger's eyes convert more Experience and Pickups to Gold.
| Torrona's Box | Mainly used for the extra Curse, increasing enemy spawns.
| Skull O'Maniac | Increases enemy spawns.
| Stone Mask | Self-explanatory.
## Strategy, Other Notes, and Tips
- Much like other builds, all the player must do is get everything and wait. Minimal input is required for this build, and is consistent with eggs.  Sarabande of Healing (VI) and  Victorian Horror (XVIII) can both scale their damage infinitely, allowing this build to scale endlessly.
- This build can make over 8 Billion Gold in 15 minutes of real time.
- Sammy is notorious for not being able to collect  Treasure Chests and Stage items. The "Popups Follow Priority" Option is extremely recommended for this reason.
- Uniting  Vicious Hunger into  Power of Lire [DLC] should be avoided, due to lower survivability and Gold conversion.
## High Eggs (1,000,000+)
- Stages and Modifiers are the same as the Low Egg build.
## Arcana
| Arcana | Note
| Stake to Your Heart (0) | Priority. Provides extra gold from every defeated enemy, acting like a constant Gold Fever.
| Victorian Horror (XVIII) | At high Golden Eggs, the vast majority of weapons become unable to kill enemies due to high Curse and capped Might. This Arcana has the best infinite damage scaling thanks to it scaling infinitely with both Amount and Greed.
## Optional Arcana
None of the below Arcana are required. They should be taken from later Arcana chests or purchased from the Merchant. 
| Arcana | Note
| Wicked Season (XIII) | 25% of the time, you will be making twice as much gold.
| Hail from the Future (X) | Make sure to seal Orologions.
| Disco of Gold (XV) | Extra Gold Fevers.
| Moonlight Bolero (VI) | Great for getting more Arcana. If taken, it should be taken early in the run. Not recommended to be taken without the "Popups Follow Priority" Option.
| Sapphire Mist (I) | Helps spawn more instances of Vicious Hunger, leading to more Gold conversion.
## Weapons and Passives
With high amounts of  Golden Eggs, Passive items become irrelevant and can be sealed to save time.
## Strategy, Other Notes, and Tips
- The overall strategy is the almost exactly the same as the low egg build, requiring a damaging Arcana to be taken to get kills.
- Every Pickup can be sealed at this point for slightly more Gold.
- Uniting  Vicious Hunger into  Power of Lire [DLC] should still be avoided.

# Guide:New player guide

This guide is currently under construction.
The information contained within should not be considered fully accurate and/or complete.
Starting out with  Antonio Belpaese and the  Whip in the Mad Forest, the first primary goal is to survive until the 30:00 mark. Breaking light sources has a chance to drop  Floor Chicken for healing, as well as  Gold Coins which can be used to purchase PowerUps that benefit future runs.
The Unlocks menu can be used as a progression path with goals to reach. Nearly all game content comes from completing unlocks, which add to the pool of available weapons and passive items that can be obtained from level up, as well as unlocking more stages to play on.
